Evidence:

- 10 of 11 sponsors have explicit Continuity prizes, but they are small and specific: The Graph $5K, 1inch $2K, Uniswap $2K, Arc ~$3.2K, World AgentKit $3.5K, Ledger $1.5K, Hedera $1K, ENS $500, Chainlink $500, Bazantic $1K → roughly **$20K (~25%) of the pot is Continuity-only**.
- No public ETHGlobal stats on fresh-vs-continuity split exist yet (searched; none found). Base-rate reasoning: hackathon culture defaults to from-scratch; Continuity requires an eligible pre-existing repo, which most of the 1,600+ hackers don't have. First-edition friction + eligibility barrier ⇒ likely single-digit-% of submissions per Continuity track competing for dedicated pots.
- Caveat: rules are strict — pre-existing work must be documented, only event-period work is judged, and "polish/bug fixes alone disqualify" (Hedera prize text). A Continuity entry must ship a genuinely new feature during Sep 4-16.
- Caveat 2: Dami's portfolio (AgentMesh, Backstop, Verdikt, x9, etc.) IS the moat here — few competitors have shipped agent-infra repos ready to extend, which is exactly what World AgentKit Continuity ($3.5K), The Graph AI Continuity ($5K), and Bazantic "Help an Agent Use Your Project" ($1K) reward.

**Play:** a Continuity entry extending an existing agent-infra repo, stacking The Graph AI (Continuity) + one payments sponsor, faces the thinnest fields with the largest dedicated pots.
