Co-owned account

Every payment needs two approvals — the agent, and LEASH. The facilitator LEASH runs enforces the limits you set, so nothing goes out that you didn't allow.

Limits in plain words

Up to $5 a payment. Up to $50 a day as a soft budget. Can pay one place only. Active during set hours.

Cut the leash

One click ends an agent's authority. Its next payment is blocked everywhere — instantly, and you can reactivate later if you change your mind.

Your fleet

Each agent hangs on an amber leash from your org. Taut means in policy. Cut the leash and it goes off everywhere.
